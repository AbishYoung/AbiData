# Code of Conduct

AbiData is built by and for people who work with data. We want everyone who takes part (in issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews and anywhere else the project is represented) to have a respectful, harassment-free experience, whatever their age, body, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, colour, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

What helps:

- Being kind and patient, especially with newcomers and people working in a second language.
- Respecting different opinions, experiences and constraints.
- Giving and accepting constructive feedback gracefully, and focusing on the work rather than the person.
- Owning mistakes, apologising to those affected, and learning from them.
- Putting what is best for the community ahead of being right.

What is not acceptable:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome sexual attention or advances.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment, including sustained disruption of discussions.
- Publishing someone's private information, such as a physical or email address, without their explicit permission.
- Any other conduct that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in all project spaces, and when someone officially represents the project elsewhere, for example by using an official account or acting as a representative at an event.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behaviour, report it privately to the maintainer, [@AbishYoung](https://github.com/AbishYoung), using the contact details on their GitHub profile. You can also use GitHub's [report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam) feature. Please include links, screenshots or other context that helps us understand what happened.

Every report is reviewed promptly and kept confidential. We will not retaliate against anyone who reports in good faith.

Security vulnerabilities are not conduct issues: report them as [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) describes.

## Enforcement

Maintainers decide on the response to a report, in proportion to its severity:

1. **Correction.** A private, written explanation of why the behaviour was inappropriate, and possibly a request for a public apology.
2. **Warning.** A warning with consequences for continued behaviour, and no interaction with the people involved for a set period.
3. **Temporary ban.** A temporary ban from any interaction or public communication with the community.
4. **Permanent ban.** A permanent ban from any public interaction within the community.

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, commits, code, issues and other contributions that do not align with this code.
