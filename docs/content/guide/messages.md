# Messages

AbiData includes built-in internal messaging so team members can hold direct conversations or discuss work inside a shared workspace without leaving the platform.

## Open Messages {#open}

- On **Your workspaces**, select **Messages** in the top bar (or open your account menu and choose **Messages**) to open your platform-wide inbox at `/messages`.
- Inside any workspace, select **Messages** in the top bar to open `/w/{ws}/messages` scoped to that workspace's threads.
- When you have unread threads that are not archived, a badge on the **Messages** button shows your unread count.

## Direct messages and workspace threads {#kinds}

Every conversation is a thread with a **Subject** and chronological replies:

- **Direct message (`direct`)**: a private conversation between you and one or more colleagues on the server, addressed by `@username`, email address or display name from the recipient directory. Only the participants on the thread can view or reply to it.
- **Workspace thread (`workspace`)**: a conversation attached to a workspace. Every member of that workspace (see [Members and roles](/docs/guide/members-and-roles)) can read and reply to the thread, including members who join the workspace later.

## Reply, mark unread and archive {#actions}

- **Reply**: write your message in the **Reply to thread** box at the bottom of the conversation and select **Send reply** (or press `Mod+Enter`). Select **Reply** on a specific message in the thread to reference that message as the parent of your reply.
- **Read and unread**: opening an unread thread marks it read automatically. Select **Mark unread** in the thread header to keep a conversation flagged for follow-up.
- **Archive**: select **Archive** to move a thread out of **Inbox** into **Archived**. When anyone posts a new reply to an archived thread, it automatically returns to every participant's **Inbox** as unread.
