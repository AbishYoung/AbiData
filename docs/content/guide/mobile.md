# Install on your phone

AbiData is an installable web app. Adding it to your home screen gives it its own icon and a full-screen window with no browser toolbar. It is still the same site: nothing extra is downloaded from an app store.

## Requirements {#requirements}

- Your AbiData address must use HTTPS. Browsers only offer installation on secure sites, with `localhost` as the one exception for testing.
- Open AbiData in Chrome (Android) or Safari (iPhone and iPad).

## Android (Chrome) {#android}

1. Open your AbiData address and sign in.
2. Open the Chrome menu (three dots).
3. Choose **Install app** (or **Add to Home screen**) and confirm.

AbiData then appears in your app drawer and on your home screen.

## iPhone and iPad (Safari) {#ios}

1. Open your AbiData address in Safari.
2. Tap the **Share** button.
3. Choose **Add to Home Screen**, then **Add**.

## Photos in the field {#photos}

On a phone, a record that has an [Attachments field](/docs/guide/tables#attachments) shows its photos on the record sheet with a large **Take photo** button. It opens the camera; the photo is uploaded and added to the record, and **Save** stores the record. Several photos can be taken one after another. The browser asks for camera access the first time.

## Offline behaviour {#offline}

AbiData always needs a connection to reach your data. When you are offline, the app opens and shows that it cannot connect instead of a browser error page. Your data, API calls and live updates are never stored on the phone by the installed app.

## Updates {#updates}

When a new version of AbiData is available you will see "A new version is available" with a **Reload** button. Reload when you are ready; an update never interrupts what you are editing.
